Our Origin

AgniFounders was founded by Varshith G S, Founder & CEO of Thiran Private Limited, with a distinct mission: to democratize access to resources, networks, and opportunities for young student builders across India.

We believe talent is evenly distributed, but opportunity is not. We find outstanding potential in non-metro engineering and arts colleges and give them the platform to fly.
