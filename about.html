Our Story

Empowering Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities with ecosystem access.

AgniFounders was born out of a stark realization: while genius and ambition are distributed evenly across the towns and colleges of India, funding, networks, and resources are highly concentrated in a few metro hubs. Student builders in non-metro engineering colleges (Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities) often work in isolation, lacking the feedback loops needed to turn code into companies.

To bridge this gap, Varshith G S founded AgniFounders (originally Udayam) as a unified movement. We exist to discover hidden engineering and product talent, connect them in a high-signal peer network, and accelerate their transition from dreamers to builders. Through workshops, hackathons, and direct capital integrations, we make startup creation a viable path for every student in India.

Core Pillars

Mission

Discover, connect, and accelerate ambitious student founders aged 16–24, specifically focusing on building functional ecosystems in Tier 2/3 cities across India.

Vision

To create a decentralized network of student-run companies that drive localized economic growth and build world-class products out of everyday colleges.

Values

High Agency, Collaborative Building, Rapid Launch Iteration, and a commitment to helping peers scale without transactional expectations.
