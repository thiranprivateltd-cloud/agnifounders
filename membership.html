How to pay — Step by step

01

Fill Application Form

Click "Apply" on your desired tier above, which reveals the registration fields. Fill out your details.

02

Pay via UPI

Scan the corresponding QR code shown next to the form or send payment directly to the UPI ID: gsv28@cnrb

03

Copy Transaction ID

Keep a copy of your UPI transaction ID/reference number from your payment app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm).

04

Submit application

Paste the transaction ID and upload your payment receipt screenshot inside the form, then click submit.

05

Onboarding

Our team reviews and validates your credentials within 24 hours. Once verified, check email for invite links.
