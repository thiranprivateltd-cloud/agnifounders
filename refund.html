Refund Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. No-Refund Policy

AgniFounders provides immediate access to digital community modules, co-founder directories, and private workspace resources (credits, templates, directories) upon membership confirmation. Therefore, all transaction fees (₹299, ₹599, or ₹999) are non-refundable and non-cancellable.

2. Failed or Duplicate Transactions

If you experience transaction failure where money was debited but your membership registration fails validation, or in cases of double-debits, please write to us immediately at thiranprivateltd@gmail.com with:

* Your registered Name & Email
* UPI Transaction reference number
* Screenshot proof of bank debit statement

Our audit team will cross-reference the Canara Bank transaction log. Once verified, duplicate payments will be refunded back to your source account within 5 to 7 working days.

3. Contact Us

For any billing inquiries, fee clearance questions, or invoice request certificates, please contact our powering coordinator at thiranprivateltd@gmail.com.
