Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you apply for membership, register for events, or contact us. This includes:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and social profile links (such as LinkedIn).
  • College/university details, startup or project summaries, and fields of interest.
  • UPI transaction references and payment receipt snapshots to validate membership dues.

2. How We Use Your Information

The collected details are strictly utilized to operate and improve AgniFounders, specifically:

  • Reviewing and processing your membership tier applications.
  • Displaying student founder cards in our public members directory.
  • Coordinating offline and online meetups, hackathons, and cohort activities.
  • Sharing partner SaaS discount codes and student developer credits.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We respect your privacy. Your data is not sold or traded with third-party advertising companies. We may share verified information only with Thiran Private Limited (our powering organization) to extend cloud infrastructure credits or mentorship services to you.

We utilize browser local storage and secure digital forms to manage application directories. We take reasonable electronic measures to protect all transaction documents and personal details.

4. Your Choices & Controls

You can request to edit or permanently delete your member card directory profile from the AgniFounders database at any time by emailing us at thiranprivateltd@gmail.com.
