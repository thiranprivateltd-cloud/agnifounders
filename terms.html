By applying to AgniFounders (under the administration of Thiran Private Limited) or accessing our community portals, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree, you must refrain from registering as a member.

2. Member Registration & Eligibility

Membership is intended for active student builders, engineers, and creators aged 16–24 in India. You must provide true, complete, and updated registration details. Registration requires payment of an annual platform maintenance fee based on your chosen tier (Spark, Builder, or Founder Pro).

3. Community Guidelines & Code of Conduct

AgniFounders hosts moderated channels. To maintain a safe and productive space for all student founders, you agree not to:

  • Spam or post unsolicited commercial advertisements.
  • Transmit harassing, abusive, or copyright-infringing materials.

Failure to adhere to these rules will result in immediate suspension from the WhatsApp community modules and deletion from the directory without a fee refund.
